Program Structure

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When does the batch start and end?+

The batch starts on Monday, 26 October 2026 and runs for 12 weeks, Monday to Saturday, finishing in mid-January 2027. The internship phase begins around 21 December 2026.

I live outside Jaipur. Can I join?+

Yes. The classroom batch is for students in Jaipur, and students from anywhere else in India join the live online batch. Online learners attend the same live sessions, do the same labs, join internship squads remotely, and receive the same certificates.

Do I need prior coding experience?+

No. Week 1 starts with Python fundamentals. Basic programming knowledge helps, and the admission assessment checks logical reasoning, not coding.

Is the internship real work?+

Yes. In Weeks 9–12 you work in a mentored squad on one of MaxBrain's AI products, following sprints, stand-ups and code reviews. Your experience letter describes the features you actually built.

Will my college accept this as an internship?+

Most colleges accept it for mandatory internship or industrial training, but rules differ. Check with your training and placement cell; we can provide an offer letter and program details for their approval.

Do you guarantee a job?+

We provide placement assistance, not a job guarantee: portfolio and resume building, mock interviews, and referrals to our client and partner network for up to 6 months after completion.

What are the batch timings?+

Morning batch 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM or evening batch 3:00 PM – 7:00 PM IST, Monday to Saturday, for both classroom and online learners.

What is the refund policy?+

Full refund minus the registration fee if you withdraw before the batch starts, 50% refund within the first 7 days, and no refund after that. One free batch transfer is allowed if requested at least 7 days before the start date.
